## ✨ Overview

Welcome to Traditional Chinese Medical Center (TCMC), dedicated to providing patient-centered care, raising awareness of Traditional Chinese Medicine, and building professional relationships with Chinese medical institutions and international healthcare organizations.

Our goal is to connect traditional Chinese medical knowledge with local healthcare needs while promoting professional development and international cooperation.

The public site currently includes **Home, About, Services, Gallery, Testimonials, and Contact**. The **Blog** and **News & Updates** sections are built in the project but are hidden from the live homepage, navigation, and footer.

## 📖 Who We Are & Our Mission

### Who We Are
Traditional Chinese Medical Center (TCMC) is a healthcare institution dedicated primarily to the practice, promotion, and development of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). Our center is committed to making traditional Chinese medical practices more accessible to the local community while promoting the exchange of medical knowledge, professional expertise, and healthcare experience.

TCMC focuses on Traditional Chinese Medicine as its core area of practice, with particular emphasis on acupuncture and other appropriate traditional therapeutic approaches. We aim to bridge traditional Chinese medical knowledge with local healthcare needs through responsible clinical practice, patient education, and professional development.

### Our Mission
Our mission is to promote and provide Traditional Chinese Medicine services, expand public awareness of TCM, and establish meaningful partnerships with Chinese medical institutions, universities, professional associations, research organizations, and relevant government agencies.

Through professional cooperation, knowledge exchange, and continuous learning, we seek to contribute to the development of traditional Chinese medical practice and its responsible application in our local healthcare environment.
